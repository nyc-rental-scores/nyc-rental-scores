How the grades work

Each listing's building is matched to the city's records by its tax lot (BBL). Every number comes from NYC Open Data. Problems are counted per apartment, then compared with all 170,000+ NYC buildings that have 3 or more apartments:

Violations: A best 40% · B next 25% · C next 20% · D next 10% · F worst 5%.
Bedbugs, evictions, complaints, landlord record: most buildings have none, so A means none on record. Buildings with any are split: B lower half · C next 25% · D next 15% · F worst 10%.

The overall grade weighs violations 30%, bedbugs 20%, evictions 20%, complaints 15%, landlord record 15%. A failing grade in any category caps the overall grade at C. Buildings in the city's Alternative Enforcement Program (its list of the most distressed buildings) get an F for landlord record. Bedbug figures are reported by owners themselves. Large complexes are graded as a whole. Grades describe public records, not the condition of any one apartment. Always check the listing and visit in person.

Data: NYC Department of Housing Preservation and Development, NYC Marshals, Department of City Planning (PLUTO, GeoSearch) via NYC Open Data. Listing details link back to the original site.
